# SAR Staff Report Presentation Profiles v1.1.0

**Presentation only.** All profiles use the same underlying facts, evidence states, uncertainty, risk methodology, security standards, and applicability logic. Organization type does not determine legal/regulatory applicability or risk. These profiles alter report emphasis/order only.

## Department

Emphasize:

- enterprise governance and ownership;
- integrations and information flows;
- information stewardship and data ownership; and
- control responsibility boundaries.

Prompt for context that clarifies which internal business/technical teams own the service, information, integrations, and ongoing oversight, and distinguish organization-managed responsibilities from vendor/shared-service responsibilities.

## Regional Center

Emphasize:

- DDS information handling and relationships;
- Business Associate responsibilities where independently established as applicable;
- vendor/service-provider relationships; and
- evidence gaps and responsibility boundaries.

Describe DDS information flows and responsible parties where supported. Do not infer HIPAA or Business Associate applicability from organization type. Keep agreement, vendor, evidence, and source-role facts separately traceable.

## Service Provider

Emphasize:

- understandable guidance for protecting potentially sensitive information;
- safe AI use and human review;
- access and administrator practices;
- retention, deletion, and disclosure; and
- practical implementation safeguards.

Explain known practices in plain language. Mark unverified safeguards as claims or unknowns; do not present general guidance as validated controls or required mitigations.

## Invariant

Changing organization type may reorder or emphasize presentation content. It must not change facts, source authority, requirements, evidence state, applicability, risk methodology, scoring, findings, approval, or execution eligibility.
