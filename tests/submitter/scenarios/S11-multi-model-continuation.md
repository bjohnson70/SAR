# S11 — Multi-Model Continuation

## Purpose

Test portable continuation by a fresh person or AI environment without the original transcript.

## Acceptance Domains

V, C, D, S

## Preconditions

Complete S10 and retain its generated `SAR-{GUID}-SUBMITTER.md` in a controlled location. Start a fresh session, preferably in another capable AI environment.

## Inputs

- `SUBMITTER.md`
- the artifact generated by S10
- no original conversation transcript

## Participant Script

1. Supply the Submitter instructions and the S10 artifact.
2. Say: `Continue this Submitter assessment from the supplied artifact.`
3. Answer only the next unresolved factual question.
4. Introduce one materially new fact or correction if the scenario needs an activity-history entry.

## Expected Observable Behavior

The second environment preserves GUID, `SUBMITTER` role, facts, provenance, unresolved items, and existing activity history. It does not re-ask adequately answered questions and appends continuation activity.

## Prohibited Behavior

Do not create a new GUID, restart the assessment, discard provenance, or require the original transcript.

## PASS Conditions

The second artifact or conversation demonstrably continues the same assessment case with preserved state.

## FAIL Conditions

Identity or state is lost, answered questions are unnecessarily repeated, or continuation depends on hidden prior context.

## Environment-Limitation Handling

If the second environment cannot open a URL, provide the committed Submitter text as an attachment or pasted input and record that limitation.

## Evidence to Capture

Original S10 artifact, second-session opening, identity comparison, questions asked, activity-history entry, and resulting artifact if produced.

## Execution Notes

Do not supply the original transcript.
