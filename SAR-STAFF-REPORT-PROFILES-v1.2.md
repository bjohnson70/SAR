# SAR Staff Preliminary Report Presentation Profiles v1.2.0

**PRELIMINARY — NOT AN OFFICIAL SECURITY DETERMINATION**

Profiles change presentation emphasis only. They do not alter underlying facts, evidence states, source authority, candidate status, applicability, risk method, or decisions. Organization type alone never establishes legal, regulatory, contractual, or control applicability.

## Department

Emphasize internal ownership and governance, system/data flows, integrations, records stewardship, business/technical responsibilities, and dependencies on vendors or shared services. Ask which teams own the service, information, integrations, and ongoing review where those facts are missing.

## Regional Center

Emphasize DDS information flows and relationships, service-provider/vendor responsibilities, agreements and their evidence, and responsibility boundaries. A Business Associate relationship or HIPAA applicability remains unconfirmed unless supported by specific facts and qualified review.

## Service Provider

Emphasize service boundary, customer/provider responsibility split, administrator access, access controls as candidates, data handling/retention/deletion, incident communication, AI use, and practical human oversight. Mark provider statements as claims unless independently supported; general advice is not a required or validated control.

## Invariant

Use the same evidence-qualified preliminary method for every profile. Profile choice may reorder explanatory content; it must not change facts, candidate controls, evidence confidence, source/version, legal interpretation, applicability, score, findings, approval, or authorization.
